cLock In

Privacy policy and support · last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

cLock In collects no data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Alarms and settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Camera. For camera challenges, frames are analysed on your iPhone while the challenge runs and discarded right away. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Motion and fitness. Step counts are read from your iPhone during a step challenge and are not stored.

What we don't do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code.
  • No data is shared with or sold to anyone, because we have none.

Permissions

The app asks for alarms, camera and motion only when a feature needs them. You can change each one at any time in iOS Settings → cLock In.

Children

cLock In collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app update that changes it, and the App Store privacy label will be updated with it.

Help

  • The alarm didn't ring. Check iOS Settings → cLock In → Alarms is allowed.
  • A challenge doesn't work right now. Tap "I can't do this one" to switch. 45 seconds after you stopped the alarm, you can also turn it off with the bouncing clocks.
